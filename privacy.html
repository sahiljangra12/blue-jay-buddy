Privacy

Your data stays with you.

Blue Jay Buddy has no accounts and no login. It never asks for your JHED password. Updated November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your plan answers, classes, deadlines you add, checklist ticks, favorites and theme are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser data, or Settings and reset → Reset everything in the app, deletes them.

Schedule uploads

When you upload an SIS schedule PDF or screenshot, it is read on your device. The file is not uploaded anywhere. The first time, your browser downloads two open-source readers (PDF.js and Tesseract.js) from public code hosts.

Canvas deadlines

If you connect Canvas, you paste your private Calendar Feed link. That link only lists your assignments; it can't log in to Canvas or change anything. It is saved in your browser. To fetch your deadlines, the link passes through a small Blue Jay Buddy helper on Netlify, because browsers can't read Canvas directly. The helper fetches the calendar and passes it straight back. It doesn't store or log the link. You can disconnect anytime. If you'd rather not use the helper, upload the downloaded calendar file instead; it's read on your device.

Usage count

Once a day, the app sends one anonymous ping so we can count how many students use it. The ping says only whether you have set up a plan (yes or no). No name, email, IP address, device ID, location or cookie is stored. Totals are public on the usage page. Browsers with "Do Not Track" turned on don't send it.

Feedback

If you use the feedback box, your comment, the type you picked, the page you were on and (only if you type it) your email address are sent by email to the student who runs this site. Delivery uses FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service, so it passes through their servers. Nothing else about you is sent. Don't include passwords or private details.

What we never collect

  • Passwords or JHED logins
  • Grades, names or student ID numbers
  • Your location
  • Advertising or tracking cookies

Links to other sites

Cards link to official Johns Hopkins pages and other services. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Blue Jay Buddy doesn't earn money from any link.
