# Blue Jay Buddy: 30-minute student test

Watch 5 students use the app. Five is enough to find most of the big problems.
Mix new and returning students, undergrad and grad, and at least one international student.

## Before you start

- Use **their own phone**, opened to the live site, in a private browser tab (so it starts fresh).
- Say: *"I'm testing the app, not you. Think out loud. If something is confusing, that's the app's fault."*
- **Don't help.** If they're stuck for more than a minute, note it and move on.
- Write down where they hesitate, what they tap first, and anything they say out loud.

## Tasks (read one at a time)

1. **"Set it up for yourself."** (Make it mine; watch whether they find it and finish.)
2. **"Add your real classes."** (Do they try the SIS upload? Does it read their schedule correctly?)
3. **"When is your next class, and how do you get there?"**
4. **"What do you have due this week?"** (Do they find Connect Canvas and the Calendar Feed link?)
5. **"Your J-Card stopped working. What do you do?"** (Search or Guides?)
6. **"It's 11 PM and you're in the library. How do you get home safely?"**
7. **"You want to print a 10-page PDF. How?"**

## After the tasks (ask all five)

1. On a scale of 1 to 10, how likely are you to use this next week? Why that number?
2. What was the most useful thing?
3. What was the most confusing thing?
4. What do you still open other apps or sites for?
5. Would you tell a friend about it? What would you say?

## After all five

- List every problem that **2 or more** students hit. Fix those first.
- Note the 1–10 scores. An average under 7 means the core experience needs work before adding features.
- Keep the quotes. They are the strongest thing to show the university.
